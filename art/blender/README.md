# Bulkhead Blender kit

All environment pieces, props and the handgun are **generated by Python scripts** in
`scripts/`. They're built with bmesh, so they're exact, repeatable and easy to tweak.
`bulkhead_kit.blend` is the saved result: open it to film or inspect the models.

## Rebuild and export

In Blender, from the MCP session or the Scripting tab:

```python
import sys; sys.path.insert(0, r"C:\repos\Bulkhead\art\blender\scripts")
import bk_build; bk_build.main()
```

This rebuilds every piece, lays out the test corridor, exports `assets/<piece>.glb`
plus `assets/kit_manifest.json`, and saves `bulkhead_kit.blend`. It takes about 5 seconds.

| Script | Contents |
|---|---|
| `bk_core.py` | Materials (the `BH_*` palette), the `Builder` (sweeps with mitred corners, boxes, cylinders, tubes, lathe, insets), stencil text, `Piece` (body / detail / decal / pipes nodes plus point lights) |
| `bk_corridor.py` | 5 corridor modules, wall profile, wall-bay details (junction box, hatch, sign, screen, cables, extinguisher), floor trough and grating, ribs, ceiling lights |
| `bk_window.py` | `corridor_straight_window`: a straight corridor with two viewports in its east wall (plate, splayed reveal, bolted bezel, gasket, `BH_ViewGlass`, sill). `build_window(parent, upto)` builds the first `upto` of its 7 modelling stages, for showing the steps |
| `bk_doors.py` | Door frame, sliding door (two leaves with small windows), bulkhead door, airlock door |
| `bk_room.py` | Room floor, coffered ceiling, wall variants A and B, wall with doorway (boolean cut), corner post |
| `bk_props.py` | Crates (3), barrels (2), O2 canister, console |
| `bk_gun.py` | BH-9 ARC energy pistol (with the `fx_muzzle` marker) |
| `bk_derelict.py` | Derelict dressing: `pipes_low` (crouch under), `pipe_burst` (steam), `debris`, `beacon` |
| `bk_arms.py` | The player's arms (gloves, armour, sleeves) in gun space; `muzzle_flash`, `bolt`, `spark`, `scorch` |
| `bk_creature.py` | The creature: 27 rigid parts with bone pivot and claw markers (`build_creature`), plus the `CREATURE_RIG` armature with IK legs and the `CR_crawl` cycle (`build_rig`, Blender only) |
| `bk_layout.py` | Places pieces the way the engine will (test corridor, room, props) |
| `bk_studio.py` | Eevee render setup, cameras, lights, isolating one piece |
| `bk_build.py` | One-shot rebuild, export and manifest |

## Conventions the engine relies on

- Units are metres. Blender is Z-up with +Y = north. glTF export converts this to Y-up with north = −Z.
- **Cell** = 4 × 4 m, origin at the cell centre on the floor. Corridor walkway is 2.4 m wide (walls at ±1.2 m); corridor ceiling 2.85 m. Room ceiling 3.4 m.
- **Corridors** are modelled in a canonical orientation: straight open N+S, corner N+E, tee N+E+S, cross all four, end N. Rotate in 90° steps (counter-clockwise turns north to west).
- Everything that crosses a cell edge (walls, trim rail, chamfer pipes, floor trough, guide lights) is identical on both walls. Any rotation of any neighbour continues it seamlessly.
- **Doors** are modelled on an edge: the local XZ plane at y = 0, passage along local Y. `door_frame` goes on every door edge, plus one leaf piece:
  - `door_sliding`: nodes `LeafL` and `LeafR`, which slide 0.8 m along ∓X.
  - `door_bulkhead` and `door_airlock`: node `Leaf`, which slides 2.4 m up.
- **Room walls** are modelled on the cell's south edge, facing north. Two walls at a room corner overlap exactly, and `room_corner` covers the joint. `room_wall_door` has the corridor-shaped opening that `door_frame` plugs.
- **Props** have their origin on the floor at the centre. The handgun's origin is at the grip; forward is +Y and up is +Z. Its rear charge display (5 segments) faces the player.
- **Handgun parts** (round 4): a solid moulded trigger guard and bladed trigger, stippled grip panels, a beavertail, a magazine baseplate and a release button (node `handgun_guard`). In the engine it is drawn as a viewmodel attached to the camera.
- **Materials**: one shared palette named `BH_*`, flat PBR values (no textures). Every material is single-sided except `BH_Glass`, which is alpha-blended. Emissive materials (`BH_Light*`, `BH_Screen*`) mark light sources.
- Corridor and room-ceiling pieces include **point lights** (KHR_lights_punctual) at the fixtures.
- **Effect markers** (rounds 6-7) are empties named `<piece>_fx_<name>`, made with `Piece.marker()`. The empty's
  +Y is the direction. The engine turns `spark*` into spark emitters, `steam*` into steam jets, and `muzzle`
  (on the handgun) into where bolts and the flash come from.
- **Derelict pieces** are modelled in a straight corridor cell's frame (travel along Y, walls at x = ±1.2). Wall
  pieces sit on the east wall, and the engine turns them 180° for the west one. `corridor_straight_broken` is
  `build_corridor('straight', broken=True)`: the same walls (same seed) with the north ceiling tile torn open.
- **Arms** share the handgun's origin and axes, so the engine draws them with the gun's own matrix.

## Video shots

The renders are in `video/blender/`:

- `hero_*.png`: 1080p finals.
- `modules/`: one card per module.
- `ui/`: Blender interface screenshots.
- `wip/`: work-in-progress renders.
- `flythrough/`: a 96-frame eye-level walk down the test corridor.
- `window/` (part 3): the viewport piece built step by step. `step_01..08.png` show the corridor,
  the cut, the plate, the reveals, the bezels, the glass, the sill and the stars
  (`bk_studio.space_world()`). `hero_window*.png` are Cycles finals, and `ui/` has a Blender
  window screenshot. The `WINDOW_STEPS` collection in the `.blend` holds the piece as built for
  the steps. To redo a step:
  `bk_window.build_window(bk_core.collection('WINDOW_STEPS'), upto=N, name='step_window')`.

The viewports go on the ship's edge only: their reveals reach x = 1.64, past the cell's wall
(x = 1.2), and the engine turns the piece so that side faces out.
