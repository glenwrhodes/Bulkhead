BULKHEAD
========

A ray-traced first-person shooter in a generated, derelict sci-fi ship.
Find the red airlock: it is the room farthest from where you start.
You are not alone. Ten of them crawl on the walls and ceilings, mostly in the
dark; watch for their amber glow. They hear footsteps: running carries far,
creeping crouched is silent. One that has seen you comes in over the walls
and ceiling, stops a few metres off, rears and hisses, then leaps. Four hits
kill one. If one reaches you, the run starts over.

The minimap (top right) is also a motion scanner. Every couple of seconds a
pulse sweeps out 25 m, and anything moving that it passes, even behind walls,
leaves a soft green blob: a direction and a rough distance, not an exact
place. The ping gets quicker, louder and higher as something closes in.
A creature that is holding still does not show.

The game has sound. Headphones help: you can hear which side the claws are on.

Run Bulkhead.exe. Nothing needs installing. Keep the "assets" folder next to
Bulkhead.exe: the ship's models are loaded from there.


WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
- Windows 10 (version 2004 or newer) or Windows 11, 64-bit.
- A graphics card with hardware ray tracing (DirectX 12 Ultimate / DXR 1.1):
    NVIDIA GeForce RTX 20-series or newer,
    AMD Radeon RX 6000-series or newer,
    Intel Arc.
  Install a current graphics driver. Without such a card the game shows a
  message and closes.


CONTROLS
--------
  W A S D / arrows   move
  Mouse              look (click the window to grab the mouse)
  Left click         fire
  Ctrl or C          crouch (to get under low pipes)
  Shift              run
  F                  flashlight on/off
  M or Tab           map (only the parts of the ship you have seen; the
                     scanner's blobs show on it too)
  R                  ray tracing on/off
  H                  HUD on/off
  N                  a new ship
  F12                screenshot (saved in a "captures" folder)
  Esc                release the mouse; press again to quit


OPTIONS
-------
  Bulkhead.exe --seed 1234       a particular ship (the same seed is the same ship)
  Bulkhead.exe --res 1920x1080   window size
  Bulkhead.exe --creatures 5     how many creatures (default 10, 0 for none, up to 24)
  Bulkhead.exe --hunt original   the creatures as they were at first: three, they
                                 don't come looking for you, and they sprint in
                                 along the floor and leap without warning
  Bulkhead.exe --help            everything else, including the headless
                                 capture, benchmark and map modes
